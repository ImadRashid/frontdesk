**Reset your password**
1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address you use for Slotwise.
3. Open the email from Slotwise and click the reset link. **The link expires after 60 minutes** and can only be used once. If it has expired, request a new one.
4. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive within 5 minutes.

**Staff who cannot log in**
The account owner can reset a staff member's password: go to **Staff → [name] → Access → Send password reset**. The owner can also resend an invitation if the staff member never accepted it.

**Two-factor authentication (2FA)**
- Turn it on in **Profile → Security → Two-factor authentication**. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy) and save your backup codes.
- Account owners can require 2FA for all staff in **Settings → Security**.

**Lost your 2FA device?**
Use one of your backup codes to sign in, then set up 2FA again. Staff members without backup codes can ask the account owner to reset 2FA from **Staff → [name] → Access → Reset 2FA**. If the account owner loses access and has no backup codes, contact support; for security we will verify ownership before making any change.

**Too many attempts**
After 5 failed attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes.
