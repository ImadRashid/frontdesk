**Check the status page first**
Live status for the booking page, dashboard, reminders, payments and calendar sync is at **status.slotwise.test**. Click **Subscribe** on that page to get incident updates by email.

**During an incident**
- Our engineering team posts an update on the status page **every 30 minutes** until the issue is resolved.
- Reminders that could not be sent during an outage are retried automatically once the service recovers.
- Bookings already made are not lost.

**If the status page shows everything is working but Slotwise isn't working for you**
1. Try a different browser or a private window, and clear the cache.
2. Check your internet connection and any firewall or ad-blocker.
3. If your booking page shows errors to clients, or nobody can book, contact support straight away with:
   - your booking page link,
   - the time the problem started,
   - a screenshot of the error, and
   - whether it affects everyone or only some staff/services.
Reports of booking pages that do not load are treated as urgent and passed to engineering.

**After an incident**
For major incidents we publish a short incident report on the status page within 5 business days.
