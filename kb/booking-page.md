Your booking page is where clients pick a service, a staff member and a time slot. Every Slotwise account gets one.

**Set it up**
1. Go to **Settings → Booking Page**.
2. Choose your page address under **Page link**. It looks like `book.slotwise.test/your-business`. You can change it at any time, but old links stop working after a change.
3. Add your logo, cover photo and a short description.
4. Under **Services**, make sure each service has a duration, a price (optional) and at least one staff member assigned. Services with no staff assigned do not appear on the page.
5. Under **Availability**, set opening hours and the booking window (how far ahead clients can book, and the minimum notice, e.g. 2 hours).
6. Turn on **Publish booking page**.

**Share it**
- Copy the link from **Settings → Booking Page → Share** and add it to your website, Instagram bio or Google Business Profile.
- Use **Embed** to get a widget code for your own website.

**Clients see "No times available"?**
Check that the staff member has working hours set in **Staff → Working hours**, that the service is assigned to them, and that their connected Google Calendar is not showing them as busy all day.
