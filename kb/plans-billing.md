**Plans (billed monthly)**
- **Starter — $29/month**: 1 staff calendar, booking page, email reminders.
- **Pro — $79/month**: up to 5 staff, SMS and WhatsApp reminders, deposits.
- **Business — $199/month**: up to 20 staff, multiple locations, advanced reports.

**Change your plan**
Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately and you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades also take effect immediately; the unused amount is applied as a prorated balance to your next invoice. Only the account owner can change the plan.

**Invoices**
All invoices are in **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Click an invoice to download a PDF. To add a company name, address or VAT number, edit **Billing details** — this applies to future invoices. For a corrected copy of a past invoice, contact the billing team.

**Update your card**
**Settings → Billing → Payment method**. If a payment fails, we retry for 7 days and email the account owner.

**Refund requests**
Refunds are not automatic. If you believe you were charged incorrectly, reply to support with the invoice number and reason. The **billing team reviews refund requests within 2 business days** and replies with a decision.

*Note for support agents: do not promise or confirm a refund, credit or free month. Pass the request to the billing team.*
