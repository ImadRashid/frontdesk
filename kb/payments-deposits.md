Slotwise uses Stripe to take card payments. Money goes straight to your Stripe account; Slotwise never holds your funds.

**Connect Stripe**
1. Go to **Settings → Payments**.
2. Click **Connect Stripe** and log in to an existing Stripe account or create a new one.
3. Finish Stripe's verification. Payouts start once Stripe has verified your business.

**Require a deposit**
1. Go to **Settings → Payments → Deposits**.
2. Turn on **Require deposit** and choose a fixed amount or a percentage of the service price.
3. Optionally limit deposits to specific services.
Clients pay the deposit on the booking page when they book. The rest is paid in person or online after the appointment.

**No-show and late-cancellation fees**
1. Go to **Settings → Payments → Cancellation policy**.
2. Set the cancellation window (for example 24 hours) and the fee.
3. Clients save a card when booking and agree to the policy. If they no-show, open the appointment and click **Charge no-show fee**. Fees are never charged automatically.

**Refunds to your clients**
You can refund a client's deposit from the appointment page (**Payment → Refund**). Refunds go back to the client's card through Stripe and take 5–10 business days to appear.

Payment disputes and payout questions are handled by Stripe.
