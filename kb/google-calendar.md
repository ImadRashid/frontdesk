Two-way Google Calendar sync shows your Slotwise appointments in Google Calendar and blocks booking slots when you are busy in Google.

**Connect**
1. Each staff member goes to **Profile → Calendar sync**.
2. Click **Connect Google Calendar** and sign in with the Google account that owns the calendar.
3. Allow both permissions (view and edit events). If you skip one, sync will only work in one direction.
4. Choose which calendars should block availability.

Changes usually appear within 5 minutes.

**Troubleshooting**
- **Appointments stopped appearing in Google.** Google sometimes revokes access after a password change or if the connection was unused for a long time. Go to **Profile → Calendar sync**, click **Disconnect**, then **Connect Google Calendar** again.
- **Double bookings.** Check that the busy calendar is ticked under "Calendars that block availability". All-day events marked "Free" in Google do not block slots.
- **Wrong times.** Make sure the time zone in **Settings → Business details** matches the time zone in Google Calendar.
- **Events from a shared calendar are missing.** Slotwise can only read calendars the connected Google account has access to.

If sync still fails after reconnecting, contact support with the staff member's name and an example appointment so our team can check the sync log.
