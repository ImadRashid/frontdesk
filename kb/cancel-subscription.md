title: Cancel your Slotwise subscription

---
You can cancel at any time yourself. There is no cancellation fee and no notice period.

**How to cancel**
1. Sign in as the **account owner** (staff accounts cannot cancel).
2. Go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**.
3. Choose a reason (optional, but it helps us improve) and confirm.

**What happens next**
- Your account stays active until the end of the current billing period. You will not be charged again.
- Your booking page goes offline at the end of the period, and no more reminders are sent.
- **Your data is kept for 30 days** after the period ends. If you reactivate within 30 days, everything is restored exactly as it was.
- After 30 days, your account data is permanently deleted.

**Before you cancel**
- Export your client list and appointment history from **Settings → Data → Export** (see "Export or delete your data").
- Tell your clients where to book in future, as your booking link will stop working.
- If you are cancelling because of price, consider downgrading in **Settings → Billing → Change plan** instead.

**Reactivate**
Sign in and click **Reactivate** on the banner within 30 days.

Cancelling does not trigger a refund of the current period. Refund questions are reviewed by the billing team.
