**Add a staff member**
1. Go to **Staff → Add staff member**.
2. Enter their name and email address.
3. Choose a role (see below).
4. Set their working hours and the services they offer.
5. Click **Send invitation**. They receive an email to set a password. Invitations expire after 7 days; you can resend from the staff member's page.

The number of staff calendars depends on your plan (Starter 1, Pro up to 5, Business up to 20). If you reach the limit, the **Add staff member** button will prompt you to upgrade.

**Roles**
- **Owner** — full access including billing, plan changes and cancelling. One per account.
- **Admin** — everything except billing and cancellation.
- **Staff** — sees and manages their own calendar and clients; cannot change settings.
- **Front desk** — can book and edit appointments for all staff, but cannot see reports or settings.

Change a role in **Staff → [name] → Access → Role**.

**Remove a staff member**
**Staff → [name] → Deactivate**. Their future appointments must be reassigned or cancelled first. Past appointments stay in your reports. Deactivated staff do not count toward your plan limit.

**Transfer ownership**
The current owner can transfer ownership in **Settings → Security → Transfer ownership**.
