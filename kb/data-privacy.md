**Export your data (self-serve)**
1. Go to **Settings → Data → Export**.
2. Choose what to export: clients, appointments, payments, or everything.
3. Click **Create export**. You will get an email with a download link (CSV files in a ZIP) within about 15 minutes. The link is valid for 7 days.
Only the owner and admins can export.

**Delete your data or a client's data**
- To delete a single client's record (for example, when a client asks you to forget them), open **Clients → [name] → Delete client**. This removes their details and anonymises past appointments.
- To delete your whole Slotwise account and all its data, or to make a formal GDPR request (access, erasure, correction), email the request to support. **These requests are handled by our privacy team and completed within 30 days.** The privacy team may ask you to verify that you are the account owner.

*Note for support agents: do not delete accounts or data yourself and do not confirm a deletion date. Pass every GDPR or deletion request to the privacy team.*

**Where is my data stored?**
Slotwise data is stored in the EU. Our data processing agreement (DPA) is available in **Settings → Data → Legal documents**.

Health information: avoid writing diagnoses or medical details in appointment notes unless your clinic's own policies allow it.
